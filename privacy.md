# Roadie privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

---

## The short version

- **Your work lives on your phone.** Your songs, voice notes, lyrics, gigs, contacts, photos and everything else you put into Roadie are stored on your phone, not on our servers. We can't see them.
- **Backups go where you choose:** your own iCloud, or a backup file you save yourself.
- **Roadie Assistant is off unless you turn it on.** When it's on, and only when you tap one of its buttons, Roadie sends a small, listed set of details to Anthropic, the company behind the AI, to write a draft for you.
- **Testing Roadie?** Feedback you send from inside Roadie comes to us, and only when you tap Send.
- **No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell anything about you.**

## Who we are

Roadie is made by Darragh O'Connor, trading as darraghoconnor.music, based in Ireland. We're the "controller" of the small amount of personal data described below, under the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Questions, or anything you'd like us to do with your data: [darraghoconnor.music@gmail.com](mailto:darraghoconnor.music@gmail.com).

## What stays on your phone

Everything you create in Roadie is kept in Roadie's own storage on your phone: songs and ideas, voice notes, lyrics and chords, setlists, gigs and other dates, venues, contacts you add, tasks, projects, instruments and their photos, and what you tell Roadie about yourself.

None of it is sent to us. Deleting Roadie from your phone deletes it, so back up first if you want to keep it.

## Backups

- **iCloud backup** (iPhone, if you switch it on): Roadie copies your work into a Roadie folder in **your own iCloud Drive**. It's stored by Apple under your Apple account, and Apple's privacy policy covers it. We can't see it.
- **Backup files:** if you make a backup file, it goes wherever you save it (Files, Dropbox, Google Drive, a computer). It's yours to look after.
- **Exporting voice notes** when clearing space works the same way: the audio goes wherever you save it.

## What Roadie asks to use on your phone

Each only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can change your mind in your phone's Settings:

- **Microphone:** to record voice notes.
- **Camera and photos:** for instrument photos, receipts and project cover art.
- **Calendars:** only if you choose a band calendar. Roadie writes your gigs and setlists into the calendar you pick, and doesn't read your other events. Whoever shares that calendar sees those entries, through your calendar provider (such as Google or Apple).
- **Notifications:** for reminders you've asked for, like gig day and tasks due. They're set up on your phone; nothing is sent to us.
- **Files:** when you choose a backup to restore or voice notes to import.

## Roadie Assistant (AI)

Roadie Assistant writes drafts for you: a plan for your week, posts about your gigs and releases, and your bio. It's **off unless you turn it on**, and Roadie shows you exactly what each action sends before you agree.

**What's sent, and only when you tap one of its buttons:**

- **Your week ahead:** your gigs, sessions, meetings and deadlines for the next 7 days (names, dates, times and venues), the tasks due, and your projects' names and release dates.
- **Gig posts:** that gig's venue, town, date and times, the act playing, your sound in your own words, the song titles on its setlist, and, after the gig, only the names you type in to thank.
- **Release post:** the project's title, type, release date and song titles, the act, and your sound in your own words.
- **Your bio:** your name, pronouns, acts, what you play, how long you've been at it, your sound, and what you're working on.
- **Changing a draft** (Shorter, Longer and so on): the draft as it stands, with your edits, and the change you picked.

**Never sent:** contacts' phone numbers and emails, fees, lyrics, voice notes, photos, your instruments, or what success means to you.

**How it travels:**

1. Your phone sends those details to Roadie's own small server, run on **Cloudflare**. It checks that only the listed details are included, passes them to Anthropic, and returns the draft. It doesn't keep the details or the draft.
2. To keep things fair and the costs in check, the server keeps a count of drafts per day against a **random ID** made on your phone when Roadie was installed. The ID isn't linked to your name, email or Apple account, and each day's count is deleted after 2 days.
3. Like any web service, Cloudflare handles your phone's internet (IP) address to deliver the request. We don't log or keep it.
4. **Anthropic** (Anthropic, PBC, in the United States) writes the draft. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic **doesn't use it to train its AI**. It deletes what's sent and the draft **within 30 days**, unless its safety systems flag a request as breaking its usage policy, in which case it may keep it for up to 2 years.

**Our legal basis** is your consent, which you give by turning Roadie Assistant on, and withdraw by turning it off in Settings → Roadie Assistant. The daily count rests on our legitimate interest in keeping the service fair and affordable.

**Outside the EU:** Anthropic is in the United States, and Cloudflare works worldwide. These transfers are covered by the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses in each company's data processing terms. Cloudflare is also certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## Beta feedback

While Roadie is being tested, testers can tell us how it's going from inside Roadie (Test Roadie, the ? on each screen, and Settings). **Nothing is sent unless you tap Send.**

**What's sent:**

- The name you give us for your feedback (asked once).
- Worked, Problem or Idea, and the note you type.
- A screenshot, if you add one. It shows whatever was on your screen, so have a look before you add it.
- Which item on the test list or which screen it's about, Roadie's version, and your phone's model and system version.
- The same **random ID** Roadie Assistant uses, so we can see which notes came from the same phone and limit how many can be sent in a day. Each day's count is deleted after 2 days.

Nothing else from Roadie goes with it: not your songs, gigs, contacts or anything else you've added.

**How it travels and where it's kept:** your feedback goes to Roadie's own small server on **Cloudflare**, and is kept in a Cloudflare database that only we can read. Once a day, a summary of the day's notes (without screenshots) is emailed to us through **Resend** and arrives in our Gmail (**Google**). Feedback never goes to Anthropic, and doesn't need Roadie Assistant to be on.

**Our legal basis** is our legitimate interest in testing and improving Roadie, which you choose to help with each time you tap Send. We use feedback only to fix and improve Roadie, and **delete it within 3 months of the beta ending**. To have yours deleted sooner, email us with the name you used.

**Outside the EU:** Cloudflare, Resend and Google may handle it outside the EU, covered by the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses in each company's data processing terms, and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the company is certified.

## Testing with TestFlight

If you're testing Roadie through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us your name and email (as you gave them when invited), the build you're using, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you send through TestFlight. Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy cover this. We use it only to fix and improve Roadie, and delete it when the beta ends.

## What we don't do

- No adverts, and no advertising IDs.
- No tracking across other apps or websites.
- No analytics or crash-reporting services inside Roadie. (Beta feedback is something you choose to send, note by note.)
- We never sell or share your information for marketing.

## Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Because your work stays on your phone, you're in control of most of it directly: change it, export it, or delete Roadie. For anything else, email [darraghoconnor.music@gmail.com](mailto:darraghoconnor.music@gmail.com) and we'll reply within a month.

If you're not happy with how we've handled something, you can complain to Ireland's **Data Protection Commission** ([dataprotection.ie](https://www.dataprotection.ie)) or the data protection authority where you live.

## Children

Roadie isn't meant for anyone under 16, and Roadie Assistant should only be switched on by someone 16 or over.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If what Roadie Assistant sends changes, Roadie asks you to agree again before it's used.
